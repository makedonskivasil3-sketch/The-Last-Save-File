# The Last Save File: a note for playtesters

Thank you for trying it. It's an unfinished game. We want to see how it plays for someone who has never seen it, so **you can't do it wrong**.

- **Play:** the link you were sent opens the game in a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). The first load can take a minute.
- **Controls:** WASD or the arrow keys to move. E to interact (hold E for some things). Esc to pause.
  - You can change the keys: Esc, then Settings, then CONTROLS.
  - A controller works too: left stick, A, Start.
- **Stopping:** stop whenever you like.
  - If it's calm, closing the tab keeps your place.
  - You can also use Esc, then Save and exit.
- **Sound:** please play with sound on, and with headphones if you can.
- **While you play,** please say out loud what you're thinking: what you're trying to do, what confuses you, what you like. The person with you won't explain anything. That's on purpose, not rudeness.
- **Afterwards** they'll ask a few short questions. There are no right answers.

We record what you do and say during the session, and nothing else. Nothing is shared with anyone outside the people making the game.
