The Last Save File:
the finish map

The whole game in play order, from the memory card to the last ending. Beside each stage: what runs alongside it (the Owner, the people, the memories and the powers), and what is still needed before that stage counts as finished. Then the road from today to a released 1.0. Read from the code at f042ae9 on claude/last-save-file-prototype-ljwuq4, 3 November 2026.

Content

Complete, as designed

  • A playable festival prologue and a memory-card cold open
  • 8 places: 7 maps plus the optional workshop
  • 5 memories, 4 truth pieces, 7 secrets
  • 6 endings, plus the Owner's purge
  • 10 glitch powers, unlocked by memories
Proven by machine

Machine-tested, not yet release-proven

  • 842 test cases; the runner now also fails on unexpected engine errors
  • All six endings are tested; the bots play through three of them
  • Windows, Linux and web builds export
  • Saves are transactional; stopping at a calm moment is free
Not proven yet

What blocks 1.0

  • No unfamiliar player has played it
  • The length is unmeasured (bots: about 2.5 min of travel; the rest is reading)
  • Code-drawn art; no capsule or key art
  • Never run on Windows hardware, a controller or a Deck
  • No store page, no demo out, no wishlists
Part 1

The story, start to finish

Main stages have solid markers and optional places dashed ones. Chapters come from story progress, not from places: whichever order a player explores in, chapters count up in order.

What the player sees and does

  • Two saves: SLOT 1 (Arin) and a deleted SLOT 2.

Prologue

The Lantern Festival

A playable memory · Player 1 is Arin, the player is Player 2

What the player sees and does

  • Light four lanterns with Arin. Each one teaches a verb: walk, interact, hide in a cone's blind side, time a spotlight.
  • The fifth lantern, by the well, needs both players to hold it. Player 1 leaves.
  • First decision: light it alone, or stay beside Arin.
  • Player 2 leaves. The session is never saved off.
  • Years pass on screen. The fifth lantern is lit, with nobody holding a controller: the first impossible act.

> ...who's in my save?

Alongside

PeopleMira watches from Arin's window. Wren and Tobben are at the festival. All three remember it later.
StorySets up the game's question: who kept the fifth lantern burning?
OwnerThe load finds an unknown entity. SLOT 2: deleted 3 years ago.

To finish

  • BuiltAll beats, skip, replay from the title ("The Festival"), story pages kept in the journal
  • PeopleDo players say "Rue" when asked who lit the last lantern? (playtest kit §8)
  • PolishThis is the trailer's hook and the store's first screenshot: give it the art pass first

The road to a full PC release before Christmas

The chosen target is a full Windows release with a working launch date of 10 December 2026. The go/no-go decision is on 4 December, made against evidence. It is a target, not a promise: Valve's onboarding and review must allow it. The game must not launch with unresolved progress loss, crashes, soft-locks or unusable controls.

Part 5

"Finished" means all of these are true

  • Every place, memory, secret and ending is in the game and tested
  • A failed save says so, keeps the last good save and retries; stopping at a calm moment is free
  • On the web, a save becomes durable only after the browser's storage sync (measured up to about 31 s on a very slow machine): proven on real low-end hardware
  • Reduced motion, reduced flashing, Owner pace, large text and key and pad remapping
  • Controls: corner assist and an early-press buffer
  • Ten or more unfamiliar players have played it, and their top problems are fixed
  • The first-ending time is measured, and the price is set from it
  • It runs on Windows hardware and a physical controller (a Deck if possible)
  • 60 fps measured on a real, mid-range graphics card
  • Capsule and key art, with the rights assigned
  • The three store moments have had their art pass; one palette across all maps
  • Screenshots, trailer and honest store text
  • A public demo with its own save; Next Fest done
  • RC passes the full suite, bots, exports and browser checks
  • Valve review approved; launch and the first patch window planned
