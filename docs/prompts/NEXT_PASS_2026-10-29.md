## What the game still lacks, by how much it matters to a player

### 1. Players can't recover the thread after a break (high)
**Problem:** a story game resumed after days needs a reminder.
- The journal exists, but nothing tells a returning player where they were or what they were doing.
- The brief already demanded that "essential context must remain recoverable".

**Build "Previously":** on Continue, after a real gap, show a short card before play resumes.
- **When:** more than 20 minutes since the last session ended, by the wall-clock stamp saved with the run.
- **Content:**
  - the current goal;
  - the last two story moments the player actually saw (journal entries, newest first);
  - who is with Rue (escort, Pip).
- **Behaviour:**
  - reader-paced and skippable;
  - holds the Owner like any story card;
  - never shown on a fresh start or straight after a death retry.
- **Tests:**
  - shown after the gap;
  - not shown without it;
  - holds threats;
  - lists only things the player has seen (no spoilers).

### 2. The late game punishes more than some players can take (high)
**Problem:** from purge 5, one death loses the save. That is the design: "the owner wins". But a narrative player who wants the story has no gentler path, and every other accessibility option exists.

**Build an "Owner pace" setting (Settings → ACCESSIBILITY):**
- **Normal (default):** unchanged.
- **Gentle:**
  - Arin walks 20% slower;
  - the static spreads 40% slower;
  - the cursor moves 20% slower;
  - the death-spot warning lasts twice as long.
- **What it must not change:** the story, purge, endings and what counts. It changes only pace.
- **Visibility:** shown in the playtest log, so findings say which pace was played.
- **Tests:**
  - each multiplier applies;
  - Normal is byte-for-byte the old behaviour;
  - the setting persists.

### 3. A broken session can't be reported usefully (medium)
**Problem:** playtesters and early players have no way to tell the author what went wrong, with what build, and where.

**Build "Report a problem" (pause menu → History):**
- **What it copies to the clipboard:**
  - the build stamp (below);
  - area, position, purge, sessions, current goal;
  - settings that change play (pace, movement variant, reduced motion and flashing);
  - the last 40 console and playtest-log lines.
- **Where it goes:** nowhere automatically. The page says to paste it into a message to the author.
- **Tests:**
  - the text has each field;
  - no personal data;
  - it works without playtest mode.

### 4. Builds can't be identified (medium)
**Problem:** playtest sheets ask for "build: version ___", but the game shows no version anywhere, so a finding can't be tied to a commit.

**Build a stamp:**
- **Source:** `res://build_info.json`, written by CI with the commit short hash and date. Locally the stamp reads "dev".
- **Where it shows:** small and muted on the title screen, and on the pause menu's History page.
- **Tests:**
  - it reads the file;
  - it falls back to "dev";
  - it never covers the menu.

### 5. Needs people, hardware or the author (cannot be built here; keep tracking)
- **Playtests:** five unfamiliar players. `docs/playtest/RUN_A_ROUND.md` is the protocol.
- **Platform:**
  - Windows executed on real hardware;
  - one physical controller, including the new pad remapping;
  - Mobile Safari (SV-2).
- **Listening:** a person listens to the mix and the voice pilot.
- **Final art and voices:** under `PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md`, with the author's authorisation and the rights.
- **Length:** measure the first ending with players. Don't pad.

### 6. Later, once the above is proven
- **Localisation readiness:** every player-facing string lives in GDScript constants with no `tr()`. That is cheap to leave and expensive to retrofit. Extract strings only once the English text is stable after playtests.
- **Content depth:** the critical path is about 2.5 minutes of travel, and the length is reading and relationships. Add places only if playtests show players want more and know what for.
