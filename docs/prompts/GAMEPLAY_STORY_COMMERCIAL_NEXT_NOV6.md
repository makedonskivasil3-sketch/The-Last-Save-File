# The Last Save File — next production milestone

Work as the lead Godot developer, game designer, narrative director, art director,
QA lead and producer. Improve an existing game through tested player-facing
changes. Do not substitute a large proposal, more lore, or more rooms for a
stronger experience. Implement one bounded milestone fully before expanding.

## 1. Establish the current truth

Fetch the latest main and branches. Read repository instructions and the current
top sections of RELEASE_READINESS, RELEASE_CHECKLIST, PRODUCT_SPEC, TRUTH_TABLE,
CANON_DECISIONS, ISSUE_REGISTER and the comprehension/fairness report dated
2026-11-04. Inspect the November 6 touch implementation and its recorded results.
Do not overwrite the gesture ownership, multi-touch, cancellation, opening Skip
and Continue repairs, including synchronous focus-loss release and portrait
Resume protection. Check exact revision/build stamps on every distribution
channel; GitHub Pages and a separately shared artifact can contain different code.

Make an issue table with evidence/revision, player impact, proposed fix, test,
status and owner. Distinguish reproduced bugs, code risks, design concerns,
production gaps and human/hardware verification gaps. Do not re-list fixed issues
as open or confuse a bot's limitation with a limitation of the actual game.

## 2. Protect the product's identity and scope

Preserve the festival, eight existing places, six endings, Godot 4.3, the Owner's
adaptation, relationships, memories and consequences. Rue is the game character
left running, not the girl. The Owner, Arin, Player 2 and Rue are distinct. Arin is
mute. The well preserves recordings rather than establishing live contact.
Native people matter independently of whether someone played them. Beliefs are
not facts. Use approved chronology and colour/choice rules.

Keep the recommended positioning: a premium PC narrative-suspense game, with a
free browser demo/playtest verified separately. Mobile is not currently a launch
target, but visible touch controls must work reliably. No new maps, endings,
engagement chores, microtransactions, voice cloning or engine migration in this
milestone. Routine reversible implementation choices do not need repeated approval.
Purchases, commissions, publishing and major creative decisions need the relevant
owner authorization; prepare concrete reviewable work before requesting it.

## 3. Prove the gameplay loop with people

The intended loop is: notice -> investigate -> deliberate action/glitch ->
readable danger -> help someone -> see a remembered consequence.

Observe 5–8 unfamiliar players where available, including some of the owner's
friends. Capture exact build, device, input and assistance. Do not explain routes
in advance. Record first useful action, wrong turns, accidental transitions,
failed interactions, repeated empty travel, understanding of danger and desire
to continue. Small samples are exploratory: report counts and observations,
not sales predictions or statistically confident percentages.

If participants are unavailable, complete the build, instrumentation and protocol
and state the missing dependency. Do not invent a playtest.

Map a fresh route and an experienced route across all eight places. Separate
walking, reading, exploration, puzzles, threats and retracing. Every mandatory
return must contain a new use, consequence, relationship beat, changed challenge
or shortcut; shorten journeys without a purpose. Evaluate whether the workshop's
well–Archive route is found and used naturally. Do not increase playtime by making
maps larger or forcing repeated dialogue. Compare movement at ordinary corners,
doors, unstable floor, interaction clusters and pursuit with supported inputs.

Audit the existing passage/exit and world-cohesion safeguards before changing
them. Build a table of every directed connection: source landmark/threshold,
destination, arrival position/facing, reason for travelling and return condition.
Check that direction, framing and lighting make the connection understandable.
An arrival must not immediately retrigger an exit or land inside a hazard or
collider. Test holding movement through a transition, rapid reversal, multi-touch
interaction while walking, and resuming near a passage. Use the existing travel
and cohesion tests; add regressions for demonstrated gaps. Preserve purposeful
non-Euclidean spaces while making their cause and the player's next action legible.

## 4. Teach the story through action

Prepare a concept map: first appearance, immediate relevance, playable example,
required understanding, reinforcement, and what remains deliberately unknown.
Audit Rue/girl, Owner/Arin, historical players/current control, recordings, slots,
awareness/purge, glitches, memories, witnesses, escorts, the Bin and safe stopping.

At each scene the player should know enough to act: what Rue wants now, why she
cares, what changed, and a practical next destination. Delay technical vocabulary
until it helps. Keep speaker and time framing consistent, including the memory
card -> past festival -> present transition. People and journal pages should
separate observed facts, claims and unresolved questions without premature reveals.

Keep the festival playable, skippable and replayable. Its shared ritual should
return as a later action whose meaning changes. Avoid repeated compulsory
flashbacks or another wholesale opening rewrite without player evidence.

For the opening route, annotate the actual sequence: shared lantern action,
partner's absence, Rue's independent act, transition to the present, an immediate
goal, first helpful relationship, first clearly warned danger, and a visible
consequence. Record what the player learns at each step and remove explanations
that compete with that step. Preserve manual reading/reveal/advance and readable
subtitle timing; completion of recorded speech must not force unread text away.

For the existing identity revelation, list three observable earlier details and
their meaning before and after the reveal. Audit who knows each fact, when they
learned it and whether a line is memory, evidence, belief or deliberate deception.
Give the reveal a playable consequence for Rue and a relationship. Preserve the
approved identities and chronology; resolve accidental contradictions explicitly.

## 5. Give each character agency and a payoff

For Rue, Wren, Mira, Tobben, Pip, the Archivist and the Owner, map motivation,
fear, contradiction, behaviour, shared action, consequence and later recognition.
Select the weakest two arcs for this milestone; do not add equal amounts of
exposition to everyone.

Inspect `scripts/story/relationships.gd` before proposing beats. Mira already
walks out, pins a note, can lose it, and has board/restoration/colour memories.
Tobben already has the guestbook, being caught going to the board, crossing out
his name, restoration and voice memories. These are implemented foundations,
not absent features. First establish which the player actually sees and what
they change; improve presentation or payoff before duplicating them.

- Rue: make inherited behaviour becoming deliberate choice playable. Preserve
  her own save press, naming the lantern, and her independent ending identity.
- Wren: protect the warmth of her game and drawings. A promise, refuge or kept
  light should visibly matter before an ending asks the player to sacrifice.
- Mira: develop her ability to act and decide; she should have value beyond being
  someone Rue transports or a source of a truth line.
- Tobben: deepen the conflict between reporting as scripted duty and choosing
  otherwise. Let the player observe and affect his behaviour.
- Pip: companionship, recognition and vulnerability come before route guidance.
- Archivist: show changed beliefs through filing, making room, and practical help,
  rather than another long explanation.
- Owner: maintain a recognisable human motivation while preserving responsibility
  for the harm. Do not excuse cruelty through an unearned speech.

Each implemented beat needs a player action, visible result and later recognition
where appropriate. Maintain distinct language, humour and temperament. Test
conversation priorities: urgent/escort/recovery dialogue must not accidentally
bury important guidance indefinitely. No arbitrary deaths or replay grind to
unlock essential emotional understanding.

For each selected arc, deliver a beat sheet with the existing trigger/state,
before and after scene, the player's action, the character's independent choice,
visible consequence, later recognition and persistent journal entry. Test refusal,
interruption, death/reset, restoration, re-entry and save/relaunch. Recognition
must match what happened: neither repeat a first meeting after a remembered
relationship nor invent an event the player did not witness. A changed character
should affect at least one encounter or practical choice, not merely add a paragraph.

## 6. Preserve meaningful endings and improve discovery

RUE is already demonstrated in one continuous playthrough by bots. It needs five
memories, two truth pieces, the completed Character File and Slot 2. Do not
reintroduce a second-run requirement or treat another sincere ending as a wrong
answer. Preserve specific witness leads and the Slot 2 commitment warning.

Measure human discovery, especially the journey away from the Edge after finding
the last memories, investigation in the Square/Archive, and the return. Give
attention-worthy clues and changed encounters rather than a spoiler checklist.
Check all six endings for preparation, agency, comprehensible consequences and
closure. Separate the identity-payoff ending from a simplistic moral ranking.

## 7. Keep failure fair and persistence trustworthy

Preserve the November 4 reload protection and persistent Edge grace. Reproduce
any remaining late-purge failures with seeds and exact state: purge, awareness,
previous deaths, static, reload position, helper availability and recovery options.
Measure what was visible and how long the player could react. Do not tune only
for bot success or suppress failed runs.

Resolve the default permanent-loss policy through a concrete owner decision:
present the current experience and a reversible recovery alternative, with
consequences for tension and player investment. Never remove the last good save
silently. Preserve transactional mission steps, unsaved feedback/retry, safe
stopping, old-save compatibility and whole-body resume checks.

Test pending gestures at dialogue, choices, cinematics, pause, focus, rotation,
scene changes and cancellation. A consumed press must not leak; a genuinely new
press must not be rejected by a frame blackout. Keep short taps, holds and
multi-touch ownership distinct. Build-only automation must not be described as
physical iPhone or controller certification.

## 8. Finish a coherent visual and audio slice

Use the Square/workshop calibration as a starting point and record approval or
specific redirection. Choose one connected section demonstrating exploration,
a character, danger, action, consequence and a quiet moment. Define silhouettes,
perspective, material detail, lighting, contact shadows, UI hierarchy and effects.
Inspect at normal game scale and maximum text settings. Make hazards, objects and
exits legible before adding detail. Character enlargement needs controlled camera,
collision, sightline and occlusion comparisons. The Edge's missing-world style
must look authored through composition, safe-surface cues and deliberate contrast.

Audit sound with human listening, including warning priority, repetitive sounds,
fatigue, silence, transitions and interruption recovery. Separate procedural
speech, operating-system TTS and licensed recorded acting. Script stability comes
before a representative small voice pilot. Arin stays mute and written Owner
notes remain written. Do not imply recordings or rights that do not exist.

Create concrete asset briefs and quotations where needed; do not buy or commission
without authorization. Preserve licences and credits. A particle/bloom pass is
not final art, and a working audio graph is not a human-approved mix.

## 9. Make commercial decisions from evidence

Define target audience, three promises and the best honest 30-second gameplay
pitch. Compare the existing Archive-entrance demo and the workshop-completion
candidate using observed understanding, completion and voluntary continuation.
Protect reveals and keep demo saves separate from the full game.

Measure real first-ending duration, interaction density and optional content.
Research comparable games with cited current sources if access exists; do not
infer precise sales from reviews. Recommend price and scope from demonstrated
length, production quality and the audience, not from an aspirational hour count.

Prepare a milestone budget with actual quotes/explicit assumptions: art, animation,
audio, QA, store materials, localisation if separately authorized, contingency and
ongoing support. Check the owner's release calendar against actual platform
requirements. A December date is a target, not evidence of readiness. Recommend
postponement or scope reduction if gates fail. No promised revenue, invented
wishlists, unapproved store publication or compulsory monetisation systems.

## 10. Verify and deliver a bounded production milestone

Declare acceptance criteria before editing. Fix the highest-impact demonstrated
issues, complete the selected character/gameplay beats, then test before expanding.
Use meaningful regressions, strict full coverage, relevant full ending routes,
rendered before/after evidence, mission-stage save/relaunch, exports and browser
checks. Verify low frame rates, real touch patterns and device/source switching.
Record the hardware for performance claims; repair misleading benchmarks before
optimising from them. Fix demonstrated causes rather than rewriting large files
because of their length. Keep the strict engine-error gate; never hide warnings
or combine selected reruns into a claimed clean full suite.

Work in a separate branch. Commit/push coherent changes, provide a reviewable PR
where access permits, and follow current merge authorization. Deliver: a short
assessment; prioritized issue register; implemented changes and player benefit;
exact passing/failing checks and remaining gaps; screenshots/video; supported
build/download and code links; and the next bounded milestone. No local path may
be represented as a public download. State where physical device, human listening,
art approval and unfamiliar-player evidence are still missing.

Success: dependable control, clear immediate purpose, characters who act and
remember, meaningful forward progress, fair danger, trusted saving, and a player
who wants to continue without the developer explaining the experience.
